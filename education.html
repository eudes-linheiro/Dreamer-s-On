01Keep asking

Make room for the next question.

Learning can turn a big, uncertain dream into smaller things you can explore. Every new question, skill, and perspective gives you another way to move forward.

Start with what interests you. Follow it at your own pace, and let what you learn shape what you try next.

ONLINE LEARNING PATHS

Choose what you want to learn.

Start with a subject that interests you. Explore the outline, try a practice activity, and keep building at your own pace.

01 / LANGUAGE

English foundations

Build confidence with everyday English, from first introductions to clear short conversations.

View course outline
  1. Greetings and introductions
  2. Useful words for daily life
  3. Listening for key details
  4. Build a clear sentence

Try it: Write three sentences introducing yourself and one thing you enjoy.

02 / LANGUAGE

French essentials

Practice useful French phrases, pronunciation, and simple conversations for everyday situations.

View course outline
  1. Bonjour: greetings and introductions
  2. Numbers, days, and useful phrases
  3. Pronunciation and listening practice
  4. Ask and answer simple questions

Try it: Practice saying “Bonjour, je m'appelle…” and introduce yourself.

03 / MUSIC

Music theory

Understand the building blocks behind rhythm, melody, scales, and chords.

View course outline
  1. Pulse, tempo, and basic rhythm
  2. Reading notes on a staff
  3. Build a major scale
  4. How chords support a melody

Try it: Tap a steady beat, then count four beats before repeating.

04 / MUSIC

Music production

Explore how to shape an idea into a track using recording, arrangement, and mixing basics.

View course outline
  1. Find an idea and choose a sound
  2. Build a beat and arrange a loop
  3. Record and balance audio layers
  4. Polish and export a first mix

Try it: Collect three everyday sounds and arrange them into a short rhythm.

A PLACE TO BEGIN

Learning starts with you.

  1. 01

    Follow a question

    Notice what you want to understand, then explore it one step at a time.

  2. 02

    Build a skill

    Practice something useful to an idea, project, or goal you care about.

  3. 03

    Share what you discover

    Talking about what you learn can give someone else a place to begin.
