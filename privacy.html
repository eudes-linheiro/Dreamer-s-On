Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Information you provide

Depending on how you use the site, Dreamer's On may receive:

  • Your name and email address or phone number when you create or update an account.
  • Your password, stored as a cryptographic hash rather than readable text.
  • Profile photos you choose to upload, courses you enroll in, and payment references you submit for course registration.
  • Your name, email address, and message when you contact us.

How information is used

Information is used to provide account access, remember course enrollments and payment-review status, verify course registration payments, display profile details, respond to contact requests, and protect the service from misuse.

Cookies and storage

When you sign in, the site uses an essential session cookie to keep you authenticated. Account, enrollment, payment-reference, and contact information is stored so the related features can work. Profile photos are stored separately from account details.

Retention and your choices

Information is kept as needed to operate the service and handle requests. To ask about information associated with your account or contact message, or to request an update or deletion, please use our contact page.

Updates and contact

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The latest version will be posted here. For privacy questions, contact us through the contact page.
